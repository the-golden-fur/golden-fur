Golden Fur — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Golden Fur ("we", "us", "our") operates the Golden Fur Management Information System (MIS), a pet grooming, hotel, and daycare booking platform. This page explains what information we collect from customers who use our Customer Portal and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create an account or book a service, we may collect:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Emergency contact and preferred communication channel
  • Pet details you provide (name, species, breed, weight class, coat type, vaccination and medical notes)
  • Booking, grooming, hotel, and daycare service history
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook: your name, email address, and profile identifier as shared by that provider

How we use this information

  • To create and manage your customer and pet profiles
  • To schedule and manage bookings for grooming, hotel, and daycare services
  • To communicate booking confirmations, reminders, and account notices
  • To let our staff provide safe and informed care for your pet (e.g. allergies, medical notes)

Third-party sign-in (Google / Facebook)

If you choose to sign up or log in using Google or Facebook, we receive the basic profile information (name and email address) that provider shares with us under your consent. If the email matches an existing Golden Fur account, that sign-in method is linked to your existing account rather than creating a duplicate one. We do not post to your Google or Facebook account on your behalf.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and is only accessible to authorized Golden Fur staff for the purpose of operating the booking platform.

Data deletion instructions

You may request deletion of your Golden Fur account and all associated personal data (profile details, pet records, and booking history) at any time, including data originally received via Google or Facebook sign-in.

To request deletion:

  • Email thegoldenfur.dev@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • We will confirm your identity, delete your account and personal data from our systems within 30 days, and reply to confirm once this is done.

Removing Golden Fur from your Facebook or Google account settings does not automatically delete data already stored with us — please also send the email above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to thegoldenfur.dev@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Golden Fur MIS Customer Portal and covers data collected via email/password, Google, and Facebook sign-in.
